## 3. Discrepancies and Observations Between Specifications and Codebase

1. **Section Header Attribution for Admin Workflows Part 2 (A-05 to A-10):**
   - *Observation:* In `media_1792221253273_d3f004c0.pdf`, Page 3 documents Chavi Maru as the author of *Admin Workflows - Part 1 (A-01 to A-04)*. Page 4 continues with *A-05 through A-10* without repeating an author header.
   - *Resolution:* In this matrix, A-05 through A-10 are documented with co-ownership across Chavi Maru, Nihar (who authored the underlying `BasePage.enterDate` logic and `A-05b` boundary test), and Smitha (whose Trainer domain is the direct beneficiary of Trainer Management).
2. **Explicit Splitting of Excel-Driven Validation (C-03 vs C-03b and A-05 vs A-05b):**
   - *Observation:* The original specification grouped boundary tests and Excel-driven tests under the same test IDs (`C-03` and `A-05`).
   - *Resolution:* In the implementation, direct programmatic boundary checks (`C-03`, `A-05b`) were separated from multi-row DataProvider iterations (`C-03b`, `A-05`), improving granular failure isolation without eliminating any original scenario.
3. **Traceability Summary:**
   - **Total Unique User Stories:** 5 (`US-01`, `US-02A`, `US-02B`, `US-03`, `US-04A/B`)
   - **Total Defined Test Cases:** 37
   - **Total Automated Test Cases:** 37 (100% Automation Coverage)
